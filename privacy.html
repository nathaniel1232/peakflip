Privacy Policy

Peak Flip · last updated 23 October 2026

The short version: Peak Flip has no accounts or passwords. To run the leaderboards, chat and cloud saves, it stores a random player ID, the name you pick, your scores, your game progress and anything you write in chat. It never asks for your real name, email or location, and your data is never sold.

Who runs Peak Flip

Peak Flip is made by Nathaniel Fiskå, who is responsible for the data described here. Questions, or requests to see or delete your data: nathanielfiska@gmail.com.

What is collected, and why

DataWhy
A random player ID made by your browserTo recognize your saves, scores and name. It isn't linked to who you are.
The leaderboard name you chooseShown next to your scores, chat messages and rider. Other players can see it.
Scores, Daily Race times and race recordingsFor the leaderboards. Your best race run is recorded (the rider's path) so other players can race your "ghost".
Game progress (coins, items, missions, settings)Backed up online so your progress isn't lost if your browser is cleared or you play on another device.
Chat messages and reportsGlobal chat messages are visible to all players for 7 days. Reported messages are kept so they can be reviewed.
Your position and messages on the Off-piste mountainSent live to other players on the same mountain so they can see you. This isn't stored.
Technical data such as your IP addressRecorded briefly in server logs by the services below, as happens with any website.

Your settings, saved game and the list of players you muted are also stored in your browser's local storage.

Services that handle this data

  • Supabase stores the leaderboards, chat, cloud saves and live Off-piste updates.
  • Google Fonts provides the title font, so your browser loads it from Google.
  • On CrazyGames: CrazyGames shows the ads and runs CrazyGames accounts. If you're logged in, your CrazyGames username is used as your leaderboard name and your progress is saved to your CrazyGames account. CrazyGames' privacy policy covers that.
  • On peakflip.io: the site is hosted by Vercel, which also counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics (no cookies). Ads come from Google AdSense, which can use cookies to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data for ads.

How long it's kept

Chat messages disappear from the chat after 7 days. Leaderboard entries, names and cloud saves are kept while the game is running, unless you ask for them to be deleted. Live Off-piste updates aren't stored at all.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without picking a name. Your scores then stay on your device and you can't chat.
  • You can change your name at any time, mute other players and report messages.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing nathanielfiska@gmail.com. Tell me your leaderboard name so I can find it.
  • If you're in the EU or EEA, the GDPR gives you these rights, and you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet).

Data is used to run the game, which is the legal basis for it. It isn't sold or shared for marketing.

Children

Peak Flip is meant for players aged 13 and over. Please don't put personal information, like your real name or where you live, in your player name or in chat.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
